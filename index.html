02. In the Lab

My work asks how a robot should perceive the parts of the world it is about to touch, when cameras only see part of the scene. Here I am collecting the real-robot demonstrations behind HapticVLA, followed by the lab's official video of the finished work.

HapticVLA · IEEE/RSJ IROS 2026 · Skoltech Top Paper Award

Teaching robots to handle fragile objects

I operated our dual-arm teleoperation rig to collect the real-world demonstrations behind HapticVLA: lifting an egg from a crate, placing waffles and cans into boxes. The policy learns tactile awareness during training and needs no touch sensor at deployment, reaching 86.7% mean success on real contact-rich tasks.

JitterVLA: A Replay Benchmark for Motion Quality in VLA-Driven Human-Robot Manipulation

Faith Ogunwoye, Didar Seyidov, Konstantin Gubernatorov, Dzmitry Tsetserukou

First author · Submitted, ACM/IEEE HRI 2027 (Short Contributions) · 2026

VLA targets reach the robot controller late and irregularly, so a sensible action can still move abruptly near people. JitterVLA replays identical commands and arrival traces through different controllers: interpolation cut modelled p99 jerk by 83% at a 2.0 cm tracking cost, and lowered measured jerk on a real UR3 in all six exploratory pairs. Code and data are withheld until the HRI review is complete. arXiv ↗ · Project page ↗

04. Projects

SmellDiffusion · First author · Under review, IEEE ICRA 2027

Finding a named gas leak with a legged robot

A quadruped is told which gas to find. An open-vocabulary olfactory scene graph keeps each gas's identity and estimated source, and diffusion planning chooses the route through the plume. As first author I built most of the simulation, evaluating the method across 424 source and wind configurations with matched Gazebo trials.

  • Diffusion planning
  • Scene graphs
  • Quadruped
  • Gazebo
Paper ↗

CloudDiffusion · IEEE SMC 2026

3D scene completion from a single camera

LiDAR is expensive, so I built the branch that replaces it with one camera: Depth Anything V2 estimates depth, which I back-project into pseudo-LiDAR for the diffusion model to complete, and then I measured exactly what that substitution costs.

  • Point clouds
