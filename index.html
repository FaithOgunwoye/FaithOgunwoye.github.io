02. Research & Projects

My work asks one question from three directions: how a robot should perceive the parts of the world it is about to touch, when cameras only see part of the scene.

HapticVLA · IEEE/RSJ IROS 2026 · Skoltech Top Paper Award

Teaching robots to handle fragile objects

I operated our dual-arm teleoperation rig to collect the real-world demonstrations behind HapticVLA: lifting an egg from a crate, placing waffles and cans into boxes. The policy learns tactile awareness during training and needs no touch sensor at deployment, reaching 86.7% mean success on real contact-rich tasks.

  • Teleoperation
  • VLA
  • Tactile learning
  • Real robots
Paper ↗

SmellDiffusion · First author · Under review, IEEE ICRA 2027

Finding a named gas leak with a legged robot

A quadruped is told which gas to find. An open-vocabulary olfactory scene graph keeps each gas's identity and estimated source, and diffusion planning chooses the route through the plume. I led the project and evaluated it across 424 source and wind configurations with matched Gazebo trials.

  • Diffusion planning
  • Scene graphs
  • Quadruped
  • Gazebo
Paper ↗

CloudDiffusion · IEEE SMC 2026

3D scene completion from a single camera

LiDAR is expensive, so I built the branch that replaces it with one camera: Depth Anything V2 estimates depth, which I back-project into pseudo-LiDAR for the diffusion model to complete, and then I measured exactly what that substitution costs.

  • Point clouds
  • Monocular depth
  • Diffusion
  • SemanticKITTI

03. Publications & Research

SmellDiffusion: Diffusion-Based Quadruped Navigation with Olfactory Scene Graphs

Faith Ogunwoye*, Iana Zhura*, Hajira Amjad, Timofei Kozlov, Didar Seyidov, Dmitrii Plotnikov, Fedor Fedorov, Dzmitry Tsetserukou

First author · Under review, IEEE ICRA 2027 · 2026

A quadruped sent to a named gas leak: open-vocabulary olfactory scene graphs keep gas identity and source estimates, and diffusion planning drives navigation, evaluated across 424 source and wind configurations with matched Gazebo trials. arXiv ↗

HapticVLA: Contact-Rich Manipulation via Vision-Language-Action Model without Inference-Time Tactile Sensing

Konstantin Gubernatorov*, Mikhail Sannikov*, Ilya Mikhalchuk*, Egor Kuznetsov, Makar Artemov, Faith Ogunwoye, Marcelino Fernando, Artem Asanov, Ziang Guo, Dzmitry Tsetserukou

IEEE/RSJ IROS 2026 · Skoltech Top Paper Award

Tactile capability is distilled into a vision-language-action policy, reaching 86.7% mean success on real contact-rich tasks with no tactile sensor at inference. I collected the real-world dual-arm teleoperation demonstrations the model was trained on. arXiv ↗

CloudDiffusion: Diffusion-Based Scene Completion in the Point Cloud Domain

Chidera Agbasiere*, Mikhail Sannikov*, Faith Ogunwoye, Erik Shaikhiev, Alex Kozinov, Ilya Mikhalchuk, Iana Zhura, Dzmitry Tsetserukou

IEEE SMC 2026

Diffusion-based LiDAR scene completion directly in the point cloud domain. I built the monocular branch, replacing LiDAR input with single-camera depth converted to pseudo-LiDAR, and quantified what that substitution costs. arXiv ↗

HapticWAM: Distilling Imagined Touch into a World-Action Model without Inference-Time Tactile Sensing

Mikhail Sannikov*, Ilya Mikhalchuk*, Konstantin Gubernatorov*, Petr Kovalev, Faith Ogunwoye, Dzmitry Tsetserukou

Under review, IEEE ICRA 2027 · 2026

A world action model that predicts future frames, imagined contact and robot actions, distilling tactile knowledge so no fingertip sensor is needed at deployment. arXiv ↗
