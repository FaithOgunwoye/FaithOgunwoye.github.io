01. About Me

I'm a mechanical engineer turned robotics and ML researcher, currently pursuing an MSc in Next Generation Communication Technologies, specializing in IoT, robotics, and CAD/prototyping.

My research interests are vision-language-action models for robot manipulation, 3D scene understanding and point cloud processing, and simulation-driven design. Recent work includes diffusion-based point cloud scene completion (PointDiffusion) and contact-rich manipulation without inference-time tactile sensing (HapticVLA).
